Scholarships
The Music Department awards several endowed scholarships to new and returning music majors every year. To be considered for a Music Scholarship, students must complete both:

a scholarship application in CSUSB’s Scholarship Manager Portal (in MyCoyote), including a letter of recommendation, a copy of your latest unoffical transcript, and a biographical summery (see below).
submit a video with three contrasting works, performed on your major instrument.
