Experiential Computing and Engaged Learning Scholarships (ExCELS)

The NSF awarded a $2.5M grant providing scholarships to undergraduate students at CSUSB's School of Computer Science and Engineering. The program targets academically talented students who demonstrate financial need pursuing computing-related majors.

Eligibility Requirements:
- Full-time enrollment (minimum 12 units per semester)
- Declared major in Computer Science, Computer Engineering, Computer Systems, Bioinformatics, or Data Science
- Cumulative GPA of 2.7 or higher
- Evidence of financial need (e.g., Pell Grant recipients)
- U.S. citizen, national, refugee, or permanent resident

Award Amount: Up to $10,000 per year per student for up to 4 years, with 30 awards available annually.

Application Deadline: Reviews begin November 1, 2025.

How to Apply: Stage 1 requires submitting the ExCELS Application Form (including ~500-word personal statement and unofficial transcript) and completing a FAFSA. Stage 2 shortlisted applicants must submit two faculty recommendation letters and an educational plan.
